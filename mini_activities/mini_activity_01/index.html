Rubik's cube competition

6:00 P.M. at 2 Elm St. Paris Ontario, Canada, October 22

A rubik's cube solving competition will be held on October 22 at 6:00 P.M.


  • Admission is $5 per person
  • Prizes are $50 for first place, $25 for second, and $5 for third
  • Win by having the fastest solve
  • Timers are provided, cubes can be borrowed if you do not have one
